Project Mission

What is this project?

In an era dominated by rapid digital transformation, rural communities often get left behind due to a lack of technical knowledge and exposure. Guided by government visions like Digital India, PM-KISAN, DigiLocker, and Aarogya Setu, this initiative acts as a targeted structural catalyst for rural inclusion.

During our intensive groundwork in Varigonda Village, student volunteers deployed multi-phase diagnostic surveys, hosted general community interactive workshops, and conducted critical one-on-one personal training modules tailored specifically for elderly citizens and women self-help groups (SHGs).

The structural outcome extends far past standard quantitative metrics: it instilled real self-reliance, built impenetrable walls against dynamic cyber fraud operations, and embedded a profound long-term civic engineering mindset within our participating student framework.

Execution Blueprint

Our Journey in the Field

1
📋

Phase 1 — Diagnosis & Survey

The team conducted door-to-door surveys across Varigonda to understand baseline internet usage. We spoke to farmers, women's SHGs, school students, and elderly residents. Key findings: only 30% owned smartphones; most had never used UPI or government portals.

2
🎤

Phase 2 — Awareness Sessions

We organized 3 community meetings and 2 school sessions. Topics covered: safe browsing, recognizing online fraud, using DigiLocker, UMANG app navigation, online banking basics, and UPI payment demos. Distributed printed Telugu-language cheat sheets for quick reference.

3
📊

Phase 3 — Execution & Training

One-on-one demo sessions were conducted for 40+ women from local SHGs on using Aarogya Setu, PM-KISAN portal, and Google Pay. Youth volunteers helped senior citizens set up DigiLocker accounts.

4
🌟

Phase 4 — Impact & Reflection

Post-session surveys showed 78% improvement in confidence using smartphones. The project strengthened teamwork, empathy, and leadership in student volunteers. Plans are underway to expand to 3 neighboring villages.

5 Golden Rules to Stay Safe Online

  • 1 Never reveal passwords, netbanking login keys, or system values to anyone.
  • 2 Always double-check caller claims before verifying banking details or transferring rewards.
  • 3 Verify secure URL indicators (HTTPS) alongside padlocks before utilizing dynamic portals.
  • 4 Avoid installing software applications directly through shared file platforms or social groups.
  • 5 Lock smartphone configurations using biometric verification loops or robust secure patterns.

OTP Fraud Alert!

Banks will never contact account holders requesting transactional OTP parameters. If someone calls asking for an OTP code to unlock an account or upgrade a card, hang up instantly.

1. Secure Registration

2. UPI PIN Management

Configure a distinct 4 or 6-digit transaction authorization key. Avoid utilizing recognizable parameters like family birthdays or easily guessed consecutive layouts.

🔍

3. Recipient Verification

Always crosscheck display titles matching vendor identities prior to executing balance dispatches via public QR scanning modules.

CRITICAL PAYMENT BOUNDARY SAFETY FOUNDATION

A UPI PIN is required solely for executing fund transfers out of an account. Receiving transfers into an account never requires a PIN entry. If a transaction demands a PIN for a reward collection, it is a scam.
